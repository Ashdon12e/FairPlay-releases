# FairPlay privacy policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

FairPlay is a music and jingle console for funfairs and travelling showmen. This policy explains what FairPlay does with your information. In short: **everything stays on your PC.** FairPlay has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your information to us or to anyone else.

## What FairPlay stores, and where

Everything is saved on your own PC:

- **Your settings:** ride groups, jingle pads, schedules, fairgrounds, playlists and similar, kept in FairPlay's own app data folder.
- **Your files:** music, jingles and documents (such as insurance or inspection papers) that you add, kept in `Documents\FairPlay`.
- **Your music library:** song names, artists and cover art read from your own music files, kept on your PC so the library loads quickly.
- **Your licence:** if you enter a FairPlay licence key, it's checked on your PC and saved there. It isn't sent anywhere.

You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling FairPlay and deleting the `Documents\FairPlay` folder.

## Microphone

FairPlay uses your microphone only while you hold the **Talk** button (or Space), so your voice can play through the speakers, with echo or reverb if you turn them on. Your voice isn't recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

## Other apps on your PC

To turn Spotify, Apple Music or your web browser down while a jingle plays, FairPlay looks at which apps are playing sound on your PC, and their window titles (which show the song that's playing). This happens only on your PC and nothing is saved or sent.

## The internet

FairPlay works without the internet. It only goes online:

- **To check for updates.** The version downloaded from GitHub checks the FairPlay download page on GitHub. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store.
- **When you choose to open a link,** such as Spotify, Apple Music or SoundCloud, which opens in your web browser or that app.

GitHub, Microsoft and those music services have their own privacy policies.

## Children

FairPlay is a tool for fairground operators and isn't aimed at children. It doesn't collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
