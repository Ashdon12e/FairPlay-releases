# FairPlay

Music and jingle console for funfairs and travelling showmen.

**Download the latest version (v0.1.3):** [FairPlay-Setup-0.1.3.exe](https://github.com/Ashdon12e/FairPlay-releases/releases/latest)

FairPlay updates itself from this page. Once it's installed you don't need to come back here.

## Update log


## 0.1.3
- **Spotify keeps going:** if the music stops on its own, FairPlay can restart it (with 10 seconds to cancel), reopen Spotify if it closed, or switch to backup music files.
- **Spotify opens with FairPlay**, and can press play for you.
- **Music control pads:** set a pad or hotkey to skip song, previous song or play/pause.
- **Pause instead of dip:** long announcements can pause the music and carry on after.
- **Spotify volume** is controlled from the Console slider.
- **Spotify setup checklist** on the Music screen.
- **No internet** warning at the top when the signal drops.
- **Fairgrounds:** save each ground's curfew, music-off time and volume limit, and switch when you pull on.
- **Sound devices:** choose which speakers or sound card and which microphone FairPlay uses.
- **Bulk upload:** add lots of jingles at once. Each pad is named after its file, and new banks are added if needed.
- **Licence terms** must be accepted on first use and after each update.
- **What's new** screen in Settings.

## 0.1.2
- Updates now come from the FairPlay download page.

## 0.1.1
- **Ride groups:** each ride has its own pads, ride timer and auto jingles.
- **Pad banks:** pages of 12 pads, switched with F1 to F8.
- Tapping a jingle that's playing restarts it from the beginning.
- **Background hotkeys:** F13 to F24 or Ctrl+Alt+1…0, Q, W, even when minimised.
- **Mic echo and reverb**, each switchable.
- **Backup** everything to one file.
- **FairPlay licence:** 14-day trial, then a licence key.

## 0.1.0
- First version: jingle pads, music from files or Spotify with automatic turn-down, ride timer, mic, schedule and auto-updates.
